Agricultural Technology

Modern & Smart Farming

Sensors in the soil, drones in the sky and satellites in orbit — discover the technologies that are making farming precise, efficient and future-ready.

The Technology Toolkit

Smart Farming Technologies

Hover the cards — each one is a tool reshaping how food is grown.

Precision Agriculture

Variable-rate seeding, fertilizer and spraying guided by field maps — inputs go exactly where they are needed.

Drones

Field scouting, crop-health imaging and precise spraying from the air — covering hectares in minutes.

GPS Farming

Auto-steer tractors and geotagged operations give straight rows, zero overlap and accurate field records.

IoT Sensors

Connected sensors stream soil, weather and machine data to your phone in real time.

Soil Moisture Sensors

Know exactly when the root zone needs water — no more guessing, no more over-irrigation.

Smart Irrigation

Sensor- and weather-triggered drip valves irrigate automatically at the right time and amount.

Satellite Monitoring

Satellite crop maps (NDVI) reveal stressed patches across large areas before they are visible on the ground.

Artificial Intelligence

AI analyses photos and data to detect pests, diseases and nutrient problems — and recommends action.

Machine Learning

Models learn from seasons of data to forecast yield, disease risk and irrigation demand.

Agricultural Robots

Robots weed, transplant, spray and even harvest — reducing labour pressure and chemical use.

Greenhouses

Climate-controlled structures grow premium vegetables and flowers year-round, regardless of weather.

Vertical Farming

Stacked indoor layers with LED lighting grow leafy greens in cities using a fraction of land and water.

Hydroponics

Soil-less farming in nutrient-rich water — clean produce, precise feeding and up to 90% water saving.

Less Waste

Water, fertilizer and pesticide are applied only when and where needed — cutting cost and environmental load.

Early Warning

Sensors, drones and satellite maps flag stress days before visible symptoms, protecting yield.

Better Records, Better Loans

Digital field records support crop insurance, credit access and smarter planning season after season.

Getting startedFarmers can begin small — soil testing services, free satellite advisory apps, weather-based agro-advisories and state agri-tech programmes are good first steps before investing in hardware.
